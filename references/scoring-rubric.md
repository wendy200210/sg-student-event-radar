# Scoring rubric

Apply hard gates before scoring. Every point needs a concrete evidence note.

## Hard gates

Reject when evidence shows that the event:

- is past, closed, sold out, or lacks a credible future registration path;
- violates the configured Singapore or online policy;
- costs more than the user's cap;
- is unrelated to configured industries, fields, roles, organisations, skills, or goals;
- is primarily admissions marketing, a generic motivation session, recruitment-service funnel, training sale, or lead-generation pitch;
- is inaccessible to the user's student stage or eligibility;
- has no specific future event page.

Use `rejection_type: hard` for factual constraint failures and `rejection_type: quality` for insufficient value.

## Score 0–10

### Goal relevance: 0–3

- 3: directly advances a configured industry, field, role, organisation, skill, or current career goal.
- 2: adjacent and clearly useful at the current student stage.
- 1: broad relevance with a plausible link.
- 0: no meaningful link.

### People access: 0–2

- 2: credible interaction with relevant practitioners, leaders, founders, researchers, hiring teams, or collaborators.
- 1: relevant speakers with Q&A, or meaningful peer interaction.
- 0: broadcast-only or no evidence of useful access.

### Concrete output: 0–2

- 2: likely portfolio artifact, interview evidence, project progress, targeted industry insight, or a specific follow-up relationship.
- 1: useful knowledge with a defined application.
- 0: attendance alone.

### Student accessibility: 0–1

- 1: eligibility and format are practical for the user's education and career stage.
- 0: eligibility is unclear, highly restricted, or poorly matched.

### Feasibility: 0–2

- 2: time, commute, registration, price, and language fit.
- 1: one manageable conflict or missing minor fact.
- 0: serious conflict or essential feasibility unknown.

## Decisions

- 8–10: high priority.
- 6–7: worth reviewing.
- Below configured threshold: ledger only.

Do not give points merely because an event is free, popular, imminent, or hosted by a famous organisation. During exam periods, reduce feasibility unless the event is remote, short, or exceptionally aligned. A strong event with a class conflict can remain visible if it still meets the threshold; expose the conflict.

## Cross-industry checks

- Finance: a risk workshop with bank practitioners and a case exercise can score highly; a generic wealth product sales seminar fails.
- Healthcare: a medtech challenge with clinician and operator feedback can score highly; a public wellness promotion unrelated to the user's goals does not.
- Consumer: a retail operations case competition with brand managers and a deliverable can score highly; a broad influencer meetup without role relevance does not.
